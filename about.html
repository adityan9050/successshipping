OUR STORY

Who We Are

Success Shipping Services (Chennai) Pvt Ltd brings nearly three decades of precision, innovation, and operational excellence to India’s major ports. We specialize in complex maritime solutions—seamlessly integrating high-volume RORO vessel operations, heavy-lift project cargo, breakbulk, stevedoring, and customs clearance under one command.

Backed by our proprietary Project Herby initiative, tech-driven tracking, and strict zero-incident SOPs, we eliminate port bottlenecks and accelerate vessel turnaround times. With over 6.5M+ vehicles handled and 15,000+ Mafi trailer moves executed, we remain the trusted logistics backbone for 672+ global partners.
